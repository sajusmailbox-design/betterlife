About Better Life

A trusted sanctuary for healing in Kottayam.

Our Philosophy

Finding the right mental health professional is the first and most crucial step toward healing. At Better Life, our practice provides a safe, confidential, and evidence-based space for personal growth and mental well-being.

Led by an experienced Consultant Psychologist and Psychotherapist holding advanced degrees in Social Work (MSW), Psychology (M.Sc.), and Counselling & Psychotherapy (M.Sc.), we bring a triple-master’s qualified expertise to every session.

A Holistic & Integrative Approach

We combine modern psychotherapy, hypnotherapy, and NLP to treat the mind, body, and emotions as a connected whole. No two minds are alike, which is why we design customized therapeutic pathways tailored specifically to your goals and life circumstances.
